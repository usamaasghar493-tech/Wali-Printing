Single Wall vs Double Wall Corrugated Boxes: Which One Do You Need?

Choosing the right corrugated box is important for protecting your products while keeping packaging costs under control. Two of the most common options are single wall and double wall corrugated boxes.

What is a single wall corrugated box?

A single wall corrugated box uses one fluted corrugated medium between two liner sheets. It is a practical choice for lightweight and moderately heavy products that need everyday protection.

  • Suitable for many retail and e-commerce products
  • Lower material use and weight
  • Cost-effective for standard packaging

What is a double wall corrugated box?

A double wall corrugated box uses two layers of fluting and three liner sheets. The extra structure provides greater stacking strength and better protection for heavier or more fragile products.

  • Better for heavier products
  • Improved resistance during transport
  • Useful when boxes will be stacked

Which option is right for your business?

The best choice depends on product weight, dimensions, fragility, shipping conditions and how boxes will be stored. Using a stronger box than necessary can increase costs, while using a box that is too weak can result in product damage.
